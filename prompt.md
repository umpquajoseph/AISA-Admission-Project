# AISA AI tech team: admissions project

The data packet is `Dutch_Bros_Techincal_Packet/` in this folder, extracted
from `Downloads\Dutch_Bros_Techincal_Packet.zip` on 2026-11-23. POS history
covers regions R04-R06 only.

## The prompt, as sent

Technical: Use a dataset we provide to build a working prototype that improves
part of the client's supply planning process.

Thank you for your patience as we prepared the next round materials. You'll
find everything below, and the deliverable is due on November 28.

For this exercise, imagine Dutch Bros has approached our team for help.

Where the client wants to get to

The client wants a planning process that takes less manual work and produces
a dependable view of upcoming supply needs.

They want to support growth and avoid shortages while limiting excess stock
and last-minute changes. They also want to incorporate new information without
losing track of what was previously planned or why expectations changed.

The planner should be able to spend their time on decisions that need
attention, with enough information to explain those decisions to regional
managers, purchasing, and finance.

Your assignment

Using the supplied data, build a working prototype that moves this process
toward the client's goal: Dutch_Bros_Techincal_Packet

Choose a useful scope for the time available and implement it. Be prepared to
demonstrate how someone would use your prototype, explain your decisions and
assumptions, and show how you checked your results.

During the interview, we will introduce new information and discuss how your
approach would respond.
